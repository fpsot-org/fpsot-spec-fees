# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT fees specification.

The specification covers software that quotes, collects, refunds or remits a fee that a public body charges under a law, in any domain: jurisdiction profiles of fee law with their sources and dates; fee schedules as dated parameter files with fee items, bases, tax treatments, exemptions, indexation steps and rounding rules; the fee quote as a decision with its lines; fee orders and payment attempts with their statuses, their status history, their settlement and the boundary that keeps card data out; refunds, remissions and disputes; inbound provider notifications and outbound events; sealed receipts; payouts and the matching of their lines; the interface of a fee engine, the contract of a provider adapter and the fixtures that test them. It includes a LinkML model, jurisdiction profiles, fee schedules, event schemas, example files and the scripts that check them.

It does not cover the processing of card payments, the choice of a payment provider, accounting ledgers beyond the export of matched entries, or the collection of debts; money, decision records, the payment status of a filing, the party, authorization, code lists, seals, audit events, APIs and secrets, which other FPSOT specifications set; the choice of a database, an engine or a product; or legal advice.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
