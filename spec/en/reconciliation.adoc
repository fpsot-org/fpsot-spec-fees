= Reconciliation
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A provider rarely sends one bank transfer for each payment.
It nets many payments, refunds and its own fees into one payout.
Some providers also deduct a fee for each payment.
A body that matches only totals cannot say which payment a missing dollar belongs to.
This page therefore matches each line of each payout to one record, to the cent.
The ledger of the body is out of scope, and only the export of matched entries is in scope.

== Payouts

[[FEE-090]]
*FEE-090.* Each payout MUST be a record of the class `Payout` of `model/fees.yaml`, with its lines.
Its gross amount MUST equal the sum of the amounts of its lines.
Its fees MUST equal the sum of the fees of its lines.
Its net amount MUST equal the gross less the fees.
A line that takes money out, such as a refund, MUST carry a negative amount.

GOV.UK Pay reports a fee and a net amount for each payment, and the net amount can be negative.
The payout of the examples nets a payment of $390.00, a refund of $40.00 and a fee of $5.85.

[[FEE-091]]
*FEE-091.* The engine MUST match each line of a payout to exactly one attempt, refund or dispute.
The kind, the provider id and the amount of the line MUST agree with that record.
A line that matches no record MUST stay `unmatched`, and the payout then has the status `exceptions`.
A payout MUST have the status `reconciled` only when every line matched.
Each attempt that a payout carries MUST then show that payout in its settlement, under FEE-044.
A line of a dispute MUST carry the amount that FEE-069 sets for its kind.

A person must explain each exception.
A provider that reverses a refund after its success sends a line that matches no refund.
That line stays an exception, under FEE-060.
The engine never closes one by itself.

[[FEE-092]]
*FEE-092.* Each attempt, refund and dispute MUST keep the id that its provider gave it.
Each order MUST keep its payment reference.
For a bank transfer, the engine SHOULD also keep the end-to-end id and the structured remittance reference.
It keeps them when the bank gives them.
An engine MUST NOT reuse an id or a reference for another record.

GovStack asks for unique ids that never roll over, for each internal and each external record.
A reused reference would match a payout line to the wrong payer.

[[FEE-093]]
*FEE-093.* The engine MUST compare amounts exactly, in the minor unit of the currency, under DAT-053 and DAT-055.
A difference MUST stay an exception.
An engine MUST NOT write a difference off as rounding.

A gap of one cent can be a fee that the provider changed without notice.

== Bank statements and the ledger

[[FEE-094]]
*FEE-094.* An engine MAY import bank statements to match payouts and bank transfers.
When it imports ISO 20022 messages, it SHOULD read the end-of-day statement camt.053.
It SHOULD also read camt.054, which notifies each credit or debit.

The page xref:alignment.adoc[Alignment with other standards] maps those messages to the records of this page.
Payments Canada reports a guide to camt.053 for its high-value system Lynx.
The research did not read it, so it is _to be verified_.

[[FEE-095]]
*FEE-095.* A body MAY export matched entries to its ledger.
An export MUST hold only lines that matched, each with the ids of its payout and of its record.
It MUST NOT post an unmatched line as a matched one.

The ledger, its accounts and its rules stay with the finance system of the body.
