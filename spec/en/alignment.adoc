= Alignment with other standards
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This page is informative, and it sets no rule.
No published standard sets the records of a public fee from its schedule to its payout.
Each source below fits one part, so this specification maps to them and claims conformance to none.
The providers on this page are examples only, and no rule requires one of them.

== GOV.UK Pay

link:https://docs.payments.service.gov.uk/api_reference/[GOV.UK Pay] is the payment platform that the United Kingdom government runs for its services.
Its eight statuses are the statuses of FEE-042, with the same field `finished`.
Its refunds have the three statuses of FEE-060, and its disputes have the four statuses of FEE-067.
The codes of this specification use kebab-case, under NAM-060, so `needs_response` becomes `needs-response`.

[cols="2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|GOV.UK Pay |This specification |Note
|`state.status` and `state.finished` |`status` and `finished` of `PaymentAttempt` |The same eight values.
|`state.code`, such as P0010 or P0020 |`failureCode` |`method-rejected`, `expired`, `canceled-by-payer`, `canceled-by-service`, `provider-error`.
|`next_url` |The page address that an adapter returns |A full redirect, under FEE-047.
|`refund_summary.amount_available` |`refundableBefore` of `Refund` |The echo that guards against two refunds at once, under FEE-061.
|`settlement_summary` |`settlement` of `PaymentAttempt` |Apart from the status, under FEE-044.
|`card_details` |`cardDisplay` of `PaymentAttempt` |Brand and last four digits only, since the adapter drops the other card fields under FEE-052.
|`reference` |`paymentReference` of `FeeOrder` |Under FEE-040.
|`fee` and `net_amount` |`fee` of `PayoutLine` |Under FEE-090.
|`Pay-Signature` |The signature that an adapter verifies |Under FEE-070.
|===

GOV.UK Pay makes a new payment for each try.
This specification groups the tries of one order, as the payment intent of Stripe does.

== GovStack Payments

The link:https://specs.govstack.global/payments/6-functional-requirements[Payments building block of GovStack] names only three transaction statuses: success, failed and in progress.
It asks for receipts, audit entries that nobody can change, and ids that never roll over.
Its reconciliation section asks for an internal id and an external id for each transaction.

[cols="2,2",options="header"]
|===
|GovStack |This specification
|in progress |`created`, `started`, `submitted`, `capturable`
|success |`success`
|failed |`failed`, `cancelled`, `error`
|Receipts and acknowledgements |`Receipt`, under FEE-080 to FEE-085
|Reconciliation ids |Provider ids and payment references, under FEE-092
|===

== ISO 20022

The link:https://web.archive.org/web/20261101175506/https://www.iso20022.org/iso-20022-message-definitions[catalog of ISO 20022] refused automated readers, and so did its code sets.
The research read the status codes in a link:https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@taprsvp/iso20022_external_codes@1.0.1/code_sets/external_payment_transaction_status_code.json[copy that a software package publishes].
Every code below is therefore _to be verified_ against the official code set.

[cols="2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Status code |This specification |Note
|RCVD, ACTC |`started` |The bank received the instruction and checked it.
|ACCP, ACFC, ACSP, PDNG, PATC |`submitted` |Checks passed or pending, with settlement in progress.
|ACCC |`success` |Settlement on the account of the creditor, which a public body should wait for.
|ACSC |`success`, for the debtor side only |A change request warns against its use for financial information.
|RJCT |`failed` |The bank rejected the instruction.
|CANC |`cancelled` |A cancellation that the sender asked for.
|===

These messages matter to a fee engine, and their versions are _to be verified_ too.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Message |Role
|camt.053 |The statement of an account at the end of the day, the main input of FEE-094.
|camt.054 |A notice of each credit or debit during the day.
|pain.001 and pain.002 |A transfer that the body starts, such as a refund by bank, and its status.
|pain.013 and pain.014 |A request to pay that the body sends, and its status.
|===

A bank transfer can stay `submitted` for days.
It reaches `success` when the provider or the statement shows the credit.

== Stripe, Moneris and PayFiP

link:https://docs.stripe.com/payments/paymentintents/lifecycle[Stripe] groups the tries of one payment in a payment intent, which returns to the first status after a decline.
Its hosted checkout is a full redirect.
Its link:https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks[notifications] carry a signature over a time stamp and the body, and arrive in no set order.

An attempt of this specification maps to one charge of a payment intent, which is one try to pay.
It never maps to the payment intent itself, since the payment intent keeps one id across all the tries.
Mapping attempts to it would give two attempts the same provider id, which FEE-092 forbids.
An adapter for Stripe reads both objects, and sets the status of the attempt from them as this table shows.

[cols="2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Stripe |This specification |Note
|A new charge, or a payment intent that waits for a payment method or a confirmation |`created` |Stripe has no status for `started`, and the adapter skips it, under FEE-043.
|Payment intent `requires_action` or `processing`, or charge `pending` |`submitted` |The payer must finish a step, or the payment is still in progress.
|Payment intent `requires_capture` |`capturable` |The engine may capture the payment or cancel it.
|Charge `succeeded` |`success` |The payment intent then shows `succeeded`.
|Charge `failed` |`failed` |With the failure code `method-rejected`, while the payment intent returns to its first status for the next try.
|Payment intent `canceled` |`cancelled` or `failed` |`cancelled` when the body canceled it, otherwise `failed` with `expired` or `canceled-by-payer`.
|===

Moneris documents a hosted checkout that it says reduces the scope of card data rules.
Its documentation is an application that the research could not read, so its details are _to be verified_.

link:https://www.collectivites-locales.gouv.fr/gerer-les-finances-publiques-locales/moyens-de-paiement/loffre-de-paiement-en-ligne-payfip[PayFiP] is the online payment service that the French public finance directorate offers to local bodies.
It takes cards and one-off direct debits, on a page that the State runs.
Its technical guides are available on request, so its statuses are _to be verified_.

== Idempotency

The link:https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-httpapi-idempotency-key-header/[draft on the Idempotency-Key header] of the {abbr-ietf-first} is still a draft.
It answers `409 Conflict` to a retry that arrives while the first request runs.
It answers `422 Unprocessable Content` to a key that comes back with another body.
API-060 to API-063 already require the key, and this specification adds nothing to them.
