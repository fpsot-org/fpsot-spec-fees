= Interfaces, adapters and conformance
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A body may change its payment provider, and a provider may change its interface.
The engine then keeps its records, and only the adapter changes.
This page sets the interface of the engine, the contract of an adapter and the fixtures that prove both.

== Interfaces

[[FEE-100]]
*FEE-100.* An interface MUST create orders, attempts and refunds with `POST` and an `Idempotency-Key`, under API-060.
It MUST NOT offer an operation that edits or deletes an attempt, a refund, a remission or a receipt.
To cancel an order or an attempt, a client MUST create a cancellation as a new resource, under API-014.
The interface MUST ask the authorization engine on every request, under IAM-050.
A provider of the interface MUST test it against its description, under TST-040.

A cancellation as a resource keeps its own id, its instant and its actor.
A `DELETE` would erase that trace.

== Adapters

[[FEE-101]]
*FEE-101.* Each adapter MUST offer these operations to the engine, or state that its provider lacks one.

* Create a payment, and return the address of the page of the provider.
* Read a payment, and capture or cancel it.
* Drop every card field of a read or a notification, under FEE-052.
* Create a refund, and read it.
* Read the disputes of a payment.
* Read the payouts, with their lines.
* Verify a notification, under FEE-070.

Each adapter MUST also publish its mapping of statuses, under FEE-051.

GOV.UK Pay, Stripe and PayFiP all fit this contract.
They differ in how they redirect, when a payment becomes final and how they net a payout.
The page xref:alignment.adoc[Alignment with other standards] shows them as examples, and requires none.

== Fixtures

[[FEE-102]]
*FEE-102.* A conformance suite of this specification MUST hold schedules, decision records and the records of fees and payouts.
It MUST also hold scenarios in English and in French, under TST-020 to TST-036.
Each scenario MUST state its dates, and name its fee items and its statuses.
A producer SHOULD test each event against the schema of its type, under TST-043.

The folder `examples/` holds the first such fixtures, and `examples/test-examples.sh` checks that the check rejects broken copies.

[[FEE-103]]
*FEE-103.* Fixtures, examples and tests MUST use synthetic data only, under TST-050.
They MUST NOT hold a card number, not even a test number that a provider publishes.
Ids of providers, payment references and bank references MUST be fictional.

A test card number in a fixture teaches a reader that card numbers may sit in records.
The examples show a card only as a brand and four digits.

[[FEE-104]]
*FEE-104.* An engine MAY offer an adapter to the Payments building block of GovStack.
Such an adapter MUST map each requirement that it claims to a rule of this specification.

[[FEE-105]]
*FEE-105.* An engine or an adapter that replaces another one MUST pass the same conformance suite, under TST-045.

A body can then change its supplier, or its provider, without a change to its records.
