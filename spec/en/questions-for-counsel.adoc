= Questions for counsel
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This page is informative.
It sets no rule, and it is not legal advice.
It lists the legal questions that this specification raises but cannot answer.
A lawyer must answer each one before {abbr-fpsot-first} or a body that charges fees relies on it.

LégisQuébec and Légifrance refuse automated readers.
The research read the Québec texts in archived copies, and read no French code.
The directive of the {abbr-tbs-first} on charging also refused automated readers.
A point marked _to be verified_ rests on a secondary source or a government page, not on the law itself.

== Fees and their basis

. *Low-materiality fees.* Sections 3 to 18 of the Service Fees Act do not apply to a fee of low materiality.
Must a quote or a receipt show that a fee is of low materiality?
The schedule of the examples marks such a fee with a flag only.

. *The cost ceiling.* Section 19 of the Financial Administration Act caps a fee at the cost of the service.
Must a schedule keep the cost basis beside each amount, or does the annual fees report suffice?

. *A declared right.* Section 112 of the Small Vessel Regulations waives the license fee for some owners.
Those owners declare a right under section 35.
The regulation speaks of an owner who can exercise the right.
A news release of Transport Canada speaks of using the craft to exercise it.
Which wording governs, and may the body ask for anything beyond the declaration?

. *Ratification in France.* A charge for a service that a decree creates lapses unless the next finance act ratifies it.
Must a French schedule record whether the finance act ratified the charge, and refuse to quote once it lapses?
The text of article 4 of the organic law on finance acts after its 2021 amendment is _to be verified_.

== Indexation and rounding

[start=5]
. *Federal rounding.* Section 17 of the Service Fees Act lets a body round an adjusted fee down.
It does so under Treasury Board policy.
Which policy sets the step, and does it bind every body?
The directive on charging is _to be verified_.

. *The Québec deferral.* Section 2 of the rounding regulation defers a fee below $5.
The fee waits until the deferred rates raise it by $0.05.
Does the test apply to the increase before rounding, as the examples assume, or after it?
For a fee of $2.00 in 2026, the raw increase is $0.041, but rounding would give $2.05.

. *The cap after 2026.* The law that caps indexation at 3% applies from 2023 to 2026.
Unless a new law extends it, do capped fees take the full rate of section 83.3 on 1 January 2027?
Its Schedule I and its amendments after May 2024 are _to be verified_.

. *Fees outside chapter VIII.1.* Section 83.11 of the Loi sur l'administration financière excludes some fees.
Those are the fees that a law, a contract or the Régie de l'énergie fixes.
How does a body show that a fee falls in one of those classes?

== Refunds and remissions

[start=9]
. *A deadline for refunds.* The federal regulations on repaying receipts set no time limit.
Does a general limitation period bound a claim for a federal refund?
What is the rule in Québec and in France, where the research found none?

. *Federal remissions.* The remission policy of Environment and Climate Change Canada rests on section 4.2.4 of the directive on charging.
It says that a remission earns no interest, goes back by the method of payment and is never 0%.
Do those terms bind every federal body, or only that department?

. *Remission in Québec.* The research found no general remission provision in the Loi sur l'administration financière.
Which law lets a Québec body remit a fee, such as the Loi sur l'administration fiscale or a sector law?

. *France.* Is a remise gracieuse of article L. 247 of the Livre des procédures fiscales the analog of a remission for a charge for a service?
Can a body refund an unused tax stamp within 18 months, as a government page reports?
Both points are _to be verified_.

== Receipts, taxes and cards

[start=13]
. *Tax content of a receipt.* What must a receipt show for a fee that bears the goods and services tax?
What must it show for the Québec sales tax?
How does the Québec sales tax apply to Québec fees at all?

. *Receipts for every order.* Federal regulations ask for a receipt on request, or when the minister directs.
Does a sealed receipt for every order raise a duty of retention or of privacy?
A receipt on request might not.

. *A page that only redirects.* FAQ 1588 says that the script criterion does not apply to a redirect.
That criterion belongs to the Self-Assessment Questionnaire A.
Does the page that starts the redirect still need any control?
Reports describe a revision of FAQ 1331.
Under it, who must accept the answer: the acquirer, the card brand or both?

. *Disputes and refunds.* A payer may ask for a refund and open a dispute for the same payment.
Which law governs the choice of the body?
May the body wait for the outcome of the dispute, as FEE-068 asks?
