= Provider notifications and events
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A payment provider tells the body about a change with a notification, often called a webhook.
Providers send each notification at least once, and in any order.
GOV.UK Pay warns that notifications may arrive out of order, and Stripe gives no order at all.
A notification is also a request from outside, which anyone can forge.
This page therefore treats a notification only as a prompt to read the payment again.
It also sets the events that the engine sends to other services.

== Inbound notifications

[[FEE-070]]
*FEE-070.* An adapter MUST verify the signature of each notification over its raw body, under the scheme of its provider.
It MUST compare signatures in constant time.
Where the scheme signs a time stamp, the adapter MUST reject a notification older than its window.
A notification whose signature does not verify MUST change nothing, and the engine MUST record it with the outcome `rejected`.

GOV.UK Pay signs the raw body with a keyed hash.
Stripe signs a time stamp and the body, and advises a window of five minutes.

[[FEE-071]]
*FEE-071.* After it verifies a notification, the engine MUST read the object that it names at the provider.
It MUST set a status only from that read, under FEE-043.
A notification MUST NOT change a status by itself.
The engine MUST record the instant of the read and its outcome, `applied` or `no-change`.

The read makes the order of arrival irrelevant.
A late notice of `submitted` finds an attempt in `success`, and the read shows `success` again.
The file `examples/permit/fees.json` holds that case, with the outcome `no-change`.

[[FEE-072]]
*FEE-072.* An adapter MUST answer a notification with a `2xx` status code once it stores it.
It MUST answer before the engine acts on it.
It MUST store the parsed record of the class `ProviderNotification`, not the raw body.
It MAY keep the body in `redactedBody`, only after it removed every field of FEE-052 from it.
The engine may already have handled the event id of a notification.
It MUST then record the notification with the outcome `duplicate`, and change nothing.

Providers retry each notification until they get a `2xx` answer.
A slow answer then brings the same notification back.
The engine must accept it twice, as API-104 asks of every consumer.
A raw body can hold the card fields that FEE-052 drops.
The adapter therefore checks the signature over the body in memory, and keeps only what it parsed.

[[FEE-073]]
*FEE-073.* An engine SHOULD read each unfinished attempt at the provider, at an interval that the operator sets.
It SHOULD do so until the attempt finishes or the provider stops tracking it.

A provider may give up on a notification after some tries.
A read on a schedule then still finds the outcome.

[[FEE-074]]
*FEE-074.* Each key that verifies notifications or calls a provider MUST be a secret under SEC-020 to SEC-024.
The endpoint that receives notifications MUST meet API-080, and its address MUST NOT carry a secret, under API-085.

Stripe lets a body roll its signing secret, with a time during which both secrets work.
An owner and a rotation period then apply to each key, under SEC-023.

== Outbound events

[[FEE-075]]
*FEE-075.* An engine MUST emit a business event under OBS-070 when an attempt, a refund or a dispute changes status.
It MUST also emit one when it issues a receipt and when it matches a payout.
An event that it sends to another service MUST be a CloudEvents event under API-100 and API-101.
The types and the schemas of their data are in `registry/events/`, under API-103.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Type |When
|`org.fpsot.fees.payment-attempt.changed` |An attempt took a new status, after a read at the provider.
|`org.fpsot.fees.refund.changed` |A refund took a new status.
|`org.fpsot.fees.dispute.changed` |A dispute took a new status.
|`org.fpsot.fees.receipt.issued` |The engine issued and sealed a receipt.
|`org.fpsot.fees.payout.reconciled` |The engine matched the lines of a payout.
|===

A remission is a decision, so its event is the event of the decisions specification under DEC-071.

[[FEE-076]]
*FEE-076.* An event of a fee engine MUST NOT hold any of these values.

* A party id.
* A payment reference.
* An amount.
* Card data, or a card field of FEE-052.

A consumer that needs one of them MUST read the record, which the interface guards under IAM-050.

An event may still carry a date, a count or a flag, as its schema shows.
Stripe calls events that carry little more than ids _thin events_.
A payment reference or an amount can lead to a person.
An event also travels further than the record it names.
