= Receipts
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

No payment standard that the research read sets the content of a receipt.
GovStack asks for receipts but leaves their content open, and GOV.UK Pay sends its own email.
A receipt of a public fee must still let the payer, an auditor and a court check what happened.
This page therefore builds the receipt from the records of the other pages.
The signed records specification then seals it.

Federal regulations on public money ask for a receipt on request, or when the minister directs.
The engine still issues one for each order, since the receipt is also the record that an audit reads.

== Issue

[[FEE-080]]
*FEE-080.* An engine MUST issue a receipt of the class `Receipt` of `model/fees.yaml` when an order reaches `paid`.
It MUST issue a new receipt after each refund that succeeds and after each remission.
A later receipt MUST name the receipt that it follows.
The kind of a receipt is `payment`, `refund`, `remission` or `no-payment-due`.

A receipt never changes, so a refund gives a new receipt.
The last receipt of an order then shows the whole story of that order.

== Content

[[FEE-081]]
*FEE-081.* Each receipt MUST hold these parts.

* The order, the payee, the payer by id and the payment reference.
* Each line of the quotes, with its fee item, its legal source and its amounts.
Each line also names its decision record and its rule version, under DEC-030 and DEC-040.
* How each tax applies to each line.
* Each successful attempt, with its amount, its method category, its provider id and its instant.
* Each successful refund so far, with its amount and its reason.
* Each remission so far, with its amount, its decision record and its legal basis.
* The total due, the total paid and the total refunded.

The rule version and the legal source on each line let a payer ask why a fee applied.
The provider id lets the body find the payment in a dispute or a reconciliation.

== Seal

[[FEE-082]]
*FEE-082.* The {term-deploying-body} MUST seal each receipt under the signed records specification, in a format of LOG-010.
The id of the receipt MUST be the id of its seal manifest, under LOG-019.
The seal profile MUST be `seal-log` or higher, under LOG-002.
A receipt MUST NOT change once sealed, under LOG-082.
A correction MUST be a new receipt that names the one that it corrects.

The log then proves when each receipt existed, and that nobody changed it later.
The payer can check a receipt with published material only, under LOG-053.

== Display

[[FEE-083]]
*FEE-083.* A receipt MUST hold codes, amounts, ids and instants, and no rendered text.
A view of a receipt MUST show the label of each code in the language of the reader, under DAT-073.
A receipt MUST NOT show a card beyond its brand and its last four digits, under FEE-046.

One sealed receipt then serves a payer in English and another in French.
The printed form MAY carry the look-up code and the QR code of LOG-056.

[[FEE-084]]
*FEE-084.* An engine SHOULD issue a receipt of the kind `no-payment-due` for an order whose amount due is zero.
That receipt shows each exemption that applied, with its legal source.

The pleasure craft license of the examples costs nothing for an owner who declared a right under section 35.
The receipt still shows the fee of $24 and the exemption, so the owner can see why.

== Taxes

[[FEE-085]]
*FEE-085.* When a line charges a tax, the receipt MUST give the registration number of the payee for that tax.
When no line charges it, each line still shows its treatment and the legal source of that treatment.

Tax law may ask more of an invoice.
Those rules are _to be verified_ for the goods and services tax and the Québec sales tax.
The research did not read those rules.
